One disposable cursor over copied/mapped input, a summary, or a retained chunk. Adapts official slice-reader semantics. Construction copies input; advancement parses records. Unsupported message delivery is rejected without advancement or terminal failure. Summary cursors support records only; snapshot Chunk cursors support messages as well as records.
